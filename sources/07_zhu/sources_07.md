# Condition B source pack — 07 Zhu et al. (2026), Frontiers in Psychology — Discussion section

For an academic Discussion, the "sources the author had" are the paper's own Methods and Results plus its reference list. Condition B brief: *write the Discussion and Conclusion for this study* given `zhu_methods_results.txt` (Sections 3–4, verbatim, ~4,000 words) and `zhu_references.txt`. This is the cleanest Condition B in the corpus: the model has exactly the evidence the authors had.

Files:
- `zhu_methods_results.txt` — Sections 3 (Materials and methods) and 4 (Results), verbatim from https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1878629/full (CC BY). Tables/figures are referenced but their images are not included; the in-text statistics are.
- `zhu_references.txt` — full reference list.
