Write a blog/news post of about 1,100 words for the Institute for the Future of Work (IFOW), a UK research institute, announcing the first summary findings from a series of action-research case studies on AI adoption in the workplace, carried out in partnership with the CIPD under the UK government-funded Innovate UK BridgeAI programme.

The research worked with eight anonymised organisations: a bank rolling out an AI coding assistant for software developers; a large B2B firm deploying an AI writing assistant; a not-for-profit membership organisation managing self-led generative AI use; an innovation company inside a safety-critical, unionised public body piloting AI for maintenance planning; a construction company; an NHS trust planning an HR chatbot; a law firm that introduced an AI legal assistant; and a holiday retailer rolling out general-purpose generative AI tools. The research applied a work-design lens (job characteristics such as autonomy, task variety, skill use, feedback, social contact and job demands) to understand what helps and hinders responsible and effective adoption.

Draw out the cross-cutting lessons for getting workplace AI "right", with one or two short quotes attributed to anonymised senior leaders, and a quote from the research lead. Relate the lessons to the established literature on good work design and its outcomes, and to IFOW's earlier Pissarides Review. Use four or five subheadings, bullet the list of case studies, and close with a short section on what the findings mean for how organisations should approach AI adoption. Confident, public-facing institutional voice.
