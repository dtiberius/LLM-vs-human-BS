Write Section 5, "Discussion", of about 1,900 words for an empirical article in Frontiers in Psychology, with the subsections 5.1 Contributions and implications for theory (with two or three numbered sub-subsections), 5.2 Methodological considerations, 5.3 Practical implications, 5.4 Limitations and future directions, and 5.5 Conclusion.

The study: a three-wave time-lagged survey (two-week intervals) of 589 university students and early-career knowledge workers in China who use generative AI tools. It distinguishes two modes of cognitive offloading to generative AI — dependent offloading (delegating core thinking to the AI) and autonomous offloading (using the AI as a scaffold while retaining control) — and tests whether the two modes are differently associated with perceived downstream cognitive functioning, through two mediating mechanisms (cognitive agency transfer and intrinsic motivation), with metacognitive monitoring as a moderator. It also examines the role of immediate perceived benefit from AI use, and includes robustness checks (a latent-variable specification, and student versus early-career subsamples). The analysis is correlational and the outcomes are self-reported appraisals.

Write in the register of the journal: interpret rather than restate results, relate findings to prior work on cognitive offloading, self-determination theory and metacognition using author–year citations, be explicit about what the design can and cannot support, and end with a conclusion paragraph that states the contribution and what further research is needed.
