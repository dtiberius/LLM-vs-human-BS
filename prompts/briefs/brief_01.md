Write a research blog post of about 1,400 words in the style of a central bank staff blog (Bank of England's Bank Underground), for economists, policymakers and economics-literate general readers.

Question: does exposure to artificial intelligence explain the decline in UK job vacancies since their 2022 peak?

The post should use UK occupational and industry vacancy data (ONS online job adverts and vacancy statistics) together with the academic and industry literature on measuring occupational AI exposure, and it should validate any exposure measure against reported business AI adoption. It should weigh AI against the competing explanations for the vacancy fall — post-pandemic over-hiring, cyclical loosening, remote working, and labour-cost changes such as National Living Wage and employer National Insurance — and say what evidence would distinguish them and what series to watch next.

Structure: short introduction; the vacancy retreat; occupational signals; industry versus occupation; broader interpretation; conclusion. Refer to two or three charts by number, each with a "Notes:" and "Sources:" line as in the house style. Cite evidence in the form Author et al (year) or organisation name. Measured, evidence-led tone; no policy recommendations. End with a one-line author affiliation placeholder.
