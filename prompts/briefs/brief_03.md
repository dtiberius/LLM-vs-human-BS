Write a research blog post of about 2,000 words for a US economic policy think tank (Economic Policy Institute house style), as the third post in a "Class of 2026" series about the labour market for young college graduates. The first post in the series looked at unemployment and participation trends for young college graduates versus young non-college workers; the second looked at the industries they work in and the overall hires rate.

This post examines the occupations young college graduates work in and whether AI exposure explains their recent labour market difficulties. Use Current Population Survey basic monthly microdata to show: the distribution of employment by major occupation group for young college graduates (ages 22–27, bachelor's degree only), all college graduates, and young non-college workers; percent change in employment by occupation since 2019 and since 2023; and the AI exposure of occupations, using a published aggregate (PCA-weighted) AI exposure score, compared across the same three groups. Review the competing published evidence on AI and entry-level employment (payroll-data studies, regional Fed analysis, the Yale Budget Lab, and critics) before presenting your own analysis. Consider labour market underutilisation using the New York Fed's tracker.

Structure: three "Key takeaways" bullets at the top; introduction linking to the earlier posts; a section on occupations and employment growth; a section on AI exposure; a concluding paragraph. Include four figures presented as titled tables or described charts, each with "Notes:" and "Source:" lines, and numbered footnotes for definitions (age range, degree definition, 12-month pooled data, O*NET, the exposure score). Plain, direct EPI tone.
