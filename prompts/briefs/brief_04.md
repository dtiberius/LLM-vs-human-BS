Write an analytical article of about 2,600 words in the house style of the UK Office for National Statistics, bringing together evidence on how UK businesses have adopted and used AI over the period from late 2023 to mid-2026. Give it a plain descriptive title of your own.

Primary source: the Business Insights and Conditions Survey (BICS), Wave 159 (5 to 28 June 2026), with a time series of AI questions running back to September 2023, and the Opinions and Lifestyle Survey (May to June 2026) for individual-level AI use. Cover: the trend in the share of businesses using at least one AI technology since late 2023 and variation by business size; the depth of adoption (number of AI technologies used, extent of use, share of employees using AI); variation by industry; comparison of employee-reported and business-reported use; the purposes AI is used for, by size band; how businesses acquire AI (free software, purchased software, in-house, outsourced); reported effects on workforce headcount, overall and by purpose of use; which job roles are affected by which AI technologies; barriers to adoption; and how businesses integrate AI skills. Set the UK figures against international evidence on adoption gaps between the US and Europe and against executive-survey measures of firm adoption, explaining why estimates differ by measurement approach. Refer to other UK government evidence on adoption motivations, management practices and innovation where relevant.

Structure with numbered sections: 1. Main points (five bullets); 2. Background; 3. Use of artificial intelligence in UK businesses; 4. Purpose and impacts of artificial intelligence use; 5. Adoption of artificial intelligence and factors limiting its use. Refer to figures by number with a figure title, a subtitle describing the measure, a "Source:" line and numbered "Notes:". Neutral, impersonal ONS tone ("we" for ONS); no policy recommendations; flag where questions are new and results are early indications.
