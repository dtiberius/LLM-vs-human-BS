Write a research blog post of about 1,700 words in the style of a central bank staff blog (Bank of England's Bank Underground), for economists, policymakers and economics-literate general readers.

Question: is artificial intelligence making the UK more productive, on the evidence of industry-level data?

Set the question in the economic history of general purpose technologies (steam, electricity, ICT) and the idea of an "invention of a method of invention", and use the ICT era as the benchmark for how a new technology's productivity effects might show up first in the sectors that produce it and later in the sectors that use it. Describe a simple framework for the channels through which AI could affect the supply side of the economy. Then examine UK evidence: ONS industry contributions to output-per-hour growth, comparing the pre-Covid decade (2010–19) with the most recent period (2023–25), and the ONS Business Insights and Conditions Survey on AI adoption by industry. Consider the AI-producing industries (computer programming and consultancy; information services) and the AI-using industries separately, and look across the whole cross-section of industries.

Structure: introduction; historical perspective; AI as a revolutionary technology and a framework; is generative AI improving UK productivity?; productivity in AI-producing industries; productivity in AI-using industries and across sectors; conclusion. Refer to one figure and three or four charts by number, each with a "Notes:" and "Sources:" line. Cite in the form Author et al (year). Measured tone; say clearly what the evidence can and cannot establish. End with a one-line author affiliation placeholder.
