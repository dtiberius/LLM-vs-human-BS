Write a thought-leadership article of about 1,200 words for the CIPD (the UK professional body for HR and people development), for HR directors, people professionals and senior business leaders, on how AI adoption is changing the way HR and leadership skills need to be applied. Give it a title of your own.

Evidence base: a survey conducted on behalf of the CIPD by YouGov between 14 January and 3 February 2026 of 1,342 people professionals and business leaders (HR professionals, HR leaders and senior leaders), benchmarking their confidence in the skills needed to support effective and responsible AI adoption — skills such as ethical data use, workforce planning, job design, reskilling, understanding AI tools' limitations, communicating AI strategy and understanding legal responsibilities. The survey also captured the organisation's stage of AI adoption, the reported impact of AI on workers' job performance, and expectations for headcount over the next 12 months. The work is part of CIPD's contribution to the UK government-funded Innovate UK BridgeAI programme.

Examine: where confidence is higher and where it is lower; how self-assessed capability relates to reported performance outcomes and to stage of adoption; how it relates to headcount expectations; why any pattern is emerging; and what it means for HR and leaders, ending with three priorities and a short conclusion. Use short paragraphs and clear subheadings; refer to one or two charts by title with a "Sample size" and "Source" line. Professional, practical CIPD tone.
